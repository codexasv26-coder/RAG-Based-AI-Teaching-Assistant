Matrices also have what's called an inverse that when multiplied with the original matrix gives us the identity matrix. We denote this with a negative 1. Graphically, this means that if we have a matrix A and we apply it to a column vector, 1-1, if we then apply the inverse of A, we'll land back on the point 1-1.
